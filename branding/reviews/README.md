# Design team reviews

The kit is overseen by a virtual design studio with a simple management chain: five specialists review in parallel, the Creative Director consolidates their reports into one decision memo, and the implementer executes that memo in a single round. Reports are kept here so every decision is traceable.

## Round 1 outcome

Implemented per the memo: mark-only sky colours (Dune / Night Gulf) so the badge always shows; thicker pin; simplified marks for 96 px and below; cleaned mono silhouette; stacked lockups with and without tagline; product lockup for Futures; single-spaced tagline; social banners respecting clear space; semantic tokens for eyebrow, success/danger text, border-strong, focus and hover; up/down arrows; navy text on green/coral badges; self-hosted fonts including Inter Italic; theme-aware logo pairs; mobile header; print rules; letterhead forced light; bracketed placeholders; slogan, name and voice rules; Office mapping in the guidelines.

Deferred to v1.1 (see memo): ink-tight wordmark bounding box, sun position nudge, font preload and metric-matched fallbacks, full component layer (inputs, alerts, nav), colour exports generated by the build script, page counters, real .potx/.dotx templates.
