# Working rules

## Never publish an exact address

Standing instruction from Alex, 3 September 2026.

**Published material gives the community, and at most the frond or sub-community. Never the plot,
villa or unit number, and never a full street address.**

| Property | Maximum published | Never published |
|---|---|---|
| Palm Jumeirah villas | `L frond, Palm Jumeirah` — prefer plain `Palm Jumeirah` | Villa or plot number, street address |
| Anywhere else | Community or sub-community | Unit number, plot number, street address, title deed reference |

"Published" means anything that leaves the desk: an Artifact page, a PDF, a brochure, a portal
entry, an email to a buyer or a broker. A desk record on this repo may hold the exact address —
the published document built from it may not.

An internal reference like `L43` **is** an exact address: frond L, villa 43. Use it for file names
and desk records; keep it out of page titles, headings and body copy.

Photography counts. Check every frame for a villa number, a gate plate, a neighbour's plate or a
street sign before a page goes out.

## Identify the property before writing about it

A reference that looks like a match is not a match. Confirm which property a label refers to with
Alex, or against a record that states it, before building anything on it. On this repo an earlier
draft of the L43 brochure was written up as a Phuket villa on the strength of a number matching an
unrelated review ID. Everything downstream of that was wrong.

## Say what is not known

Where a figure has not been established, the record holds `null` and the page says so. `null` means
"not known", never "none". A brochure that quietly omits a missing field reads as though the field
does not apply.
